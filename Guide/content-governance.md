# Campus 360 — Editorial, Commercial & Data Governance

## Trust model

Trust is a product feature.

Campus 360 earns it through:
- visible timestamps;
- source discipline;
- accuracy;
- transparent Corrections;
- clear sponsorship;
- consistent editorial behavior.

## Editorial independence

Commercial can influence:
- Campaign existence;
- deliverables;
- scheduling obligations;
- disclosure.

Commercial cannot silently alter:
- facts;
- reporting;
- headlines;
- source notes;
- editorial conclusions.

Editors own journalism.

## Sponsored content

Use clear labels:
- Sponsored
- Paid Partnership
- Presented by
- Supported by

Do not disguise paid content as ordinary editorial recommendation.

## Sources

Sources may be:
- public;
- confidential;
- anonymous;
- official;
- eyewitness;
- documentary.

Private source identity/contact never leaks to:
- public API;
- Search;
- analytics;
- client bundle.

Anonymous/confidential source publication requires appropriate approval.

## Verification

Separate from workflow:
- Unverified
- In Progress
- Partially Verified
- Verified
- Official Confirmation
- Disputed

Ordinary published reporting generally requires Verified/Official Confirmation.

Breaking override is restricted and audited.

## High-risk stories

Examples:
- death;
- sexual misconduct allegations;
- criminal accusations;
- defamation risk;
- serious institutional allegations;
- sensitive investigations;
- legal threats.

Require EIC approval and legal review where needed.

## Corrections

Do not silently rewrite material factual errors.

Correction captures:
- wrong info;
- correct info;
- reason;
- severity;
- editor;
- time;
- public note.

Minor grammar/punctuation need not clutter page.

## Developing stories

Preserve chronology.

If initial information changes materially:
- add clear update;
- correct if necessary;
- do not pretend the earlier state never existed.

## Events

Postponed/cancelled:
- state explicitly;
- preserve context;
- do not silently swap date.

## Opportunities

Before publish:
- verify organization;
- source;
- application;
- eligibility;
- deadline;
- scam/payment risk.

Paid promotion does not bypass verification.

Expired:
- closed;
- removed from active;
- direct URL may remain.

## Vendors

Verification:
- business identity;
- contact;
- location;
- category;
- policy.

Featured status is disclosed.

Campus Guide does not imply every listing is an editorial “best” recommendation.

## Media rights

Capture:
- creator;
- credit;
- owner;
- license/source;
- restrictions.

Unknown-rights = warning.

Do not publish an image merely because it was found online.

## User submissions

Story Tips, Opportunity submissions and Vendor applications do not auto-publish.

They enter review.

Tip acknowledgement:
- receipt does not guarantee publication;
- newsroom may contact if details were provided.

## Privacy

Collect only what is necessary.

Do not require:
- national ID;
- exact home address;
- registration number;
- date of birth

to consume public content.

“My Campus” does not require precise GPS.

## Internal least privilege

Commercial users do not need confidential sources.

Editorial users do not automatically need campaign budgets/contracts.

## Retention

Define retention for:
- staff;
- Contributors;
- commercial contacts;
- Tips;
- audit;
- analytics;
- future public accounts.

Legal retention periods must be confirmed before production.

## Public policies

Launch:
- Privacy
- Terms
- Editorial Standards
- Corrections Policy
- Advertising/Sponsored Policy
- Contact
- Submit a Tip

## Archive/delete

Prefer Archive over hard deletion for editorial history.

Hard delete is rare/permission-controlled.

Deactivated Contributors retain authorship attribution.
